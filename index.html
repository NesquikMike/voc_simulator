Vote of Confidence

A sitting of the House of Commons

Be the Prime Minister. Survive the vote of confidence.

You have three turns to put a policy to the House. This is usually a hung or knife-edge parliament: pick your own party’s median and you will likely lose rebels, and still need votes from elsewhere.

Your Chief Whip will estimate the division, name MPs who are on the fence, and tell you how many of your own backbenchers are expected to rebel. You have three ministerial posts to offer for the whole sitting — spend them on the ambitious waverers in your party, or save them.

At the end of the third round (or earlier if you divide), 250 ayes carry the confidence motion. The House is generated afresh each game.
